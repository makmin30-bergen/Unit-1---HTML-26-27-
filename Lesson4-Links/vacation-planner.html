Trip Research

I have heard about Italy for a while and I always wanted to go there. The official Italy tourism site (opens in new tab) has information about different villages, landscapes, and landmarks such as Fermo, Nieve, or Oliena. I also read what the reviews are for many locations in italy on Italy's TripAdvisor page (opens in new tab) To make sure the weather is good for a trip, I checked the weather in Rome, Italy (opens in new tab)

Transportation Info

To get around Italy, it is reccomended to take Trenitalia (opens in new tab) since it provides fast transport. You should also have a digital map (opens in new tab) to navigate around the cities.
