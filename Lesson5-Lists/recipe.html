Apple Sponge Cake

I picked this apple sponge cake recipe because my family prepares it all the time on holidays and other special occasions. Unlike a lot of sponge cake recipes, this recipe is not loaded with sugar and mainly gets its sweetness from the apples.

Ingrediants

  • 6 large eggs
  • 1 cup of granulated sugar
  • 1 1/3 cup of all purpose flour
  • 1/2 teaspoon of baking powder
  • 1 teaspoon of vanilla extract
  • 1 1/2 lbs of medium or large apples

Steps

  1. Preheat the oven to 350 degrees fahrenheit.
  2. Line the bottom of an ungreased 9 inch springform pan with parchment paper
  3. Whisk the eggs in a stand mixer on high speed for 10 minutes.
  4. Peel, core, quarter, and slice the apples
  5. Whisk together the flour and baking powder in a medium size bowl
  6. Sift the flour mixture into the egg while folding
  7. Make sure there are no streaks of flour and that you didn't over mix
  8. Fold in the vanilla extract
  9. Put the mixture in the pan and add the apples on top
  10. Finally, bake for 50 minutes

Notes

  • Equipment Needed
    • A 9 inch springform pan
    • A stand mixer
    • A medium and a small bowl
  • Serving suggestions
    • Serve like you would normally a sponge cake.
    • Sprinke a bit of powdered sugar on top of the cake
